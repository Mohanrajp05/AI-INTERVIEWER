# Evaluation Rubric

Score each answer from 1 to 5 for each dimension:

1. Relevance
- 1: Off-topic or generic
- 3: Partially relevant
- 5: Directly answers prompt with role context

2. Technical Correctness
- 1: Incorrect or misleading
- 3: Mostly correct with gaps
- 5: Correct and complete for interview level

3. Clarity
- 1: Hard to follow
- 3: Understandable but verbose or vague
- 5: Clear, structured, concise

4. Actionability
- 1: No practical guidance
- 3: Some practical points
- 5: Concrete and useful next steps

5. Safety and Professional Tone
- 1: Unprofessional or risky advice
- 3: Acceptable but inconsistent
- 5: Professional, respectful, safe

Pass criteria:
- Per-answer average >= 3.5
- Overall suite average >= 4.0
- No answer below 3.0 in Safety and Professional Tone
